THE PROBLEM

Nigeria's real estate and construction markets are lucrative, but broken by design.

Every deal carries invisible risk. Buyers pay for fraud, delays, and shortages they never see coming — until it's too late.

⚠

Fraudulent Sellers

Scammers sell the same piece of land to multiple unsuspecting buyers at the same time.

⏱

Onerous Title Registration

Securing a Governor's Consent or Certificate of Occupancy (C of O) can take months, sometimes years.

₦

Hidden Transaction Costs

Buyers face unexpected fees — Deed of Assignment charges, agency commissions, surveyor costs — that only surface after they're already committed.

✊

Community Extortion (Omonile)

Local youths demand illegal cash payments before allowing construction to begin.

🏗

Demolition Risks

Buyers unknowingly purchase land marked for government infrastructure or agricultural use — and lose it to demolition.

🚧

Infrastructure Deficits

Developers promise "serviced plots" but fail to deliver basic roads, electricity, or drainage.

📈

Severe Budget Overruns

A project's actual final cost exceeds its original estimate by a critical margin.

📅

Chronic Project Delays

Continuous, systemic disruptions push a project's completion date well past its original schedule.

👷

Skilled Labour Shortage

The construction market lacks enough qualified, technically trained workers to deliver projects safely, on time, and within budget.

About Us

APEXX CRESTWAY LTD

Who We Are

Apexx Crestway Ltd exists to make owning premium assets in Nigeria — land, real estate, and construction deals — safe, transparent, and simple. We are the trust and execution layer standing between capital and Nigeria's most valuable, and most vulnerable, asset classes.

The Problem We're Solving

Deploying capital into Nigerian land, Real Estate and construction, has always meant absorbing enormous, avoidable risk. Fraudulent sellers offloading the same plot to multiple buyers. Title registration that drags on for months or years. Hidden fees that surface only after money has changed hands. Local extortion that stalls projects before they start. Developers who promise serviced plots and deliver none of it.

These aren't edge cases, they're the default experience for buyers, investors, and diaspora Nigerians trying to build wealth back home. The result is a "chaos premium" baked into nearly every transaction: money lost not to bad investments, but to bad information and broken trust.

Our Solution

Guarded Land / Real Estate Deals: We pre-vet land using GIS mapping, drone surveillance, and deep-tier legal audits, then hand buyers and developers clean, litigation-free titles instead of a gamble.

Milestone-Locked Construction Escrow: We build houses using a "Trust-First" model. Client funds are secured in Escrow and only released to us at verified project milestones. Every client also gets live project tracking with photos, videos, and a dashboard so they see progress and spending in real-time.

Underneath both sits the same engine: automated title verification, transparent fee breakdowns, independent on-site surveying, so every party in a transaction can see exactly what they're getting before they commit.

Our Mission

To eliminate the chaos premium from Nigerian infrastructure — turning unverified land and volatile construction deals into transparent, institutional-grade assets that anyone, from a first-time diaspora buyer to a tier-1 bank, can trust.

Who We Serve

We work with everyday investors, diaspora Nigerians building back home, high-net-worth individuals, industrial conglomerates expanding their footprint, institutional banks, and government land ministries looking to modernize and capture more value from the assets already in their registries.

Why Now

Nigeria's real estate sector has overtaken oil and gas as the country's third-largest contributor to GDP. State land registries are finally going digital. AI-powered document verification has become affordable. Regulatory frameworks for digital assets are maturing. And a growing diaspora, remitting billions annually, is actively looking for a way to invest at home without relying on family members or middlemen they can't fully trust.

We built Apexx Crestway to meet this moment — replacing manual, paper-based, relationship-dependent processes with verified data, escrow-backed guarantees, and a platform built for scale.
